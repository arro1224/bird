# Natural decoration references

The shipped wetland decoration is redrawn as resolution-independent Flutter
paths in `lib/bird_companion/core/widgets/natural_backdrop.dart`. No third-party
bitmap or SVG file is bundled.

Visual references consulted on 2026-07-19:

- Openclipart, “Reeds Silhouette” by GDJ:
  https://openclipart.org/detail/242951/reeds-silhouette
- Wikimedia Commons, “SVG silhouettes of birds” category:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_silhouettes_of_birds

The project painter uses original simplified reed stems, water arcs and generic
two-curve flying-bird marks. It does not trace or embed the referenced files.
